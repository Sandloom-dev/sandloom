# Sandloom concept map

Status: draft, 2026-11-02, not yet approved by the owner

Built with `sandloom-docs-concepts` steps 1–4. Sources, in order: the public names of `src/sandloom/sdk/` and their tiers (`__beginner__` in `sandloom/sdk/__init__.py` and `sandloom/sdk/testing/__init__.py`, plus the `client` fixture: 29 names), `uv run sandloom --help` and its subcommands, `guarantees.yml` (G-/C- areas), the error-code families in `docs/reference/error-codes.md` (C static, B startup, R runtime, T gates), `docs/getting-started/glossary.md`, and the nav in `zensical.toml`. Levels follow `.claude/skills/sandloom-docs-writing/audience.md`; plain words follow the Naming section of `positioning.md` and its inventory's plain-words column.

60 concepts: 8 `new`, 17 `first-app`, 23 `building`, 12 `operating`. One root, `Application`. A home is a page path under `docs/`, with the section anchor when the concept is one section of a longer page.

## Learning path

1. `new`: Application, Module, Module isolation, Toolchain, Check, Generator, Agent tooling, Deployment topology
2. `first-app`: Module layout, Gate, SDK and beginner names, Model, Unit of work, Authenticated route, Tenant scoping, Migration, Module communication, Event, Consumer, Generated files, Contract, Test kit, Capability, Driver, Standard module
3. `building`: Route policy, Authorization policy, Outbox and inbox, After-commit effect, Command, Idempotent write, Effect deduplication, Billing gate, Audit record, Event versioning, Projection, Saga, Background task, Row-level security, Session revocation, Instance, Module context, Architecture graph, Standard-module customization, Admin console, Write admission, Request lifecycle, Guarantee and label
4. `operating`: Deploy profile, Operations, Service split, Service RPC, Preflight, Dead letters and replay, Keys and secrets, Backup and restore, Upgrade, Data extraction, Incident response, Driver authoring

Within a level the order puts each concept after its needs and its parent, and the most used first. Across levels it is the reading order from Overview to production.

## Structure check

- **Tree.** Every concept has one parent, `Application` is the only root, and every parent appears before its children in the learning path (checked by script over the table).
- **Needs.** No cycle (topological sort over the Needs column succeeds). Every need sits at the same or a lower level than the concept that needs it.
- **Homes.** Every home path exists under `docs/`; every anchor matches a heading on that page.
- **Siblings (M3–M4).** Under `Unit of work` the children split by one criterion (what rides on a unit's commit). Two splits mix criteria and are listed under Gaps: the children of `Application` and of `Module`.

## Questions for the owner

1. **Levels of what the tutorial teaches.** The skill sets a concept's level at the earliest level a reader meets it; the tutorial (`first-app`) teaches `db()`/units, events, consumers, contracts and `TenantScoped` in place, while audience.md lists unit, event, contract, consumer and tenant as `building` vocabulary. I read audience.md as "used without explanation at `building`" and put these concepts at `first-app`. Is that right, or should they be `building` and the tutorial explain them only as plain words?
2. **Kernel at `new`.** I made Kernel a `new` concept because "How Sandloom works" (Intro) draws it, with the plain words "the framework runs the hard parts for you". The other reading folds it into Application and keeps the word for `operating`/contributor pages. Which?
3. **Outbox home.** I chose modules/database/units.md (publishing is atomic with the write) over modules/between/events.md (delivery and dead letters). Which page owns the outbox?
4. **Agent tooling as a concept.** I listed the generated `AGENTS.md`, skills and `llms.txt` as one `new` concept with no home yet. The other reading treats them as part of the project structure, not a concept. Should it get its own page?
5. **A `Toolchain` node.** Adding one concept over Check, Generator and Agent tooling would make the root's children split by one criterion; I left it out to keep the tree flat. Add it?
