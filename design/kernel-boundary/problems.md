Evidence lives in [baseline.md](baseline.md) (commit b8d8506), [guarantee-map.md](guarantee-map.md), [validation.md](validation.md), [perf-dx-baseline.md](perf-dx-baseline.md) and the gate probe at HEAD `86aed8b` ([`_evidence/roadmap-review/gate-probe.txt`](_archive/_evidence/roadmap-review/gate-probe.txt), cases G1–G5); invariants `I-xx` in [requirements.md](requirements.md). This file lists problems, not solutions: the solutions are decided in the ADRs.

## Root causes

- **R1 Responsibility boundary.** The kernel provides generic mechanisms and also owns part of the standard modules' domain model.
- **R2 Ownership under independent versioning.** Interfaces and settings of modules are owned and shipped by the kernel.
- **R3 Trust model.** The trust in security claims and in the sources of rights is not defined by a mechanism.
- **R4 Verifiability.** Some guarantees are written down but not enforced by tests or checks.

Not every problem needs a new mechanism: removing R1–R4 removes most of them. The most dangerous ones are #1 (partly fixed by PR #70; the authority assignment is open) and #13; the most fundamental are #2–#5.
