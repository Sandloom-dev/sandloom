The test harness no longer needs `CACHE_URL` / `BROKER_URL` in `.env.test` or the shell. `SANDLOOM_TEST_CACHE_URL` / `SANDLOOM_TEST_BROKER_URL` keep their names and are merged into `INFRA` (every declared instance with a Redis `url` / `broker`, plus the `default` cache and task queue whose driver takes that url from the flat setting) and are copied onto `CACHE_URL` / `BROKER_URL` only where those are still set. `sandloom_cache` and `real_uow_client` read the cache url of `INFRA`'s `cache.default` (the flat `CACHE_URL` is the fallback), and the connection checks and the beat check read the urls of `INFRA` instances.
