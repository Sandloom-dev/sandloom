### Changed

- **The test harness needs no `CACHE_URL` / `BROKER_URL`** (idk-flat-urls-s0b). `SANDLOOM_TEST_CACHE_URL` and `SANDLOOM_TEST_BROKER_URL` keep their names and are merged into `INFRA`: every declared instance with a Redis `url` (the cache url) or `broker` (the broker url), and the `default` cache and task queue whose driver takes that url from the flat setting (created when `INFRA` names none). They are copied onto `CACHE_URL` / `BROKER_URL` only where `.env.test` still sets those. `sandloom_cache` and `real_uow_client` read the cache url of `INFRA`'s `cache.default` (the flat `CACHE_URL` is the fallback), and the self-connection check and the beat check read the urls of `INFRA` instances. `sandloom.core.testing` additions only (`workers.infra_with_overrides`, `infra.resolved_cache_url`, `families.INSTANCE_URL_OPTIONS`).
