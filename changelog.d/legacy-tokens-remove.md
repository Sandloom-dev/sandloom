### Breaking changes

- **The `legacy_tokens` compatibility mode is removed** (legacy-tokens-remove). A verifier always behaves as ADR-0014 decision 4 stage 3: a token without `cpv` gives no rights where a policy needs a protected claim (401 `token_policy_outdated`), and one refresh at an issuer returns a token with `cpv`. Removed: `LegacyClaimsTokens` (`sandloom.sdk.app`), the `legacy_tokens` argument of `create_app`, the `claims.legacy_tokens` block of `/diagnostics/core`, the `claims` section of `sandloom doctor` (`--skip claims` is refused as an unknown section), the `claims_policy_legacy_tokens_total` metric, and the deploy-profile check's agreement on `legacy_tokens` with its adoption-release gate (SANDLOOM-T014 keeps every other invariant). The guarantee G-RISK-14 is retired. `previous=` and `cpv` stamping are unchanged. Upgrade: delete `legacy_tokens=LegacyClaimsTokens(...)` from the composition root, remove `claims` from any `sandloom doctor --skip` list, and roll the issuers and verifiers of a deployment out from the same release; a token minted before `cpv` existed gets one 401 and refreshes.
