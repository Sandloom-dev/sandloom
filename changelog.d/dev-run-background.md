### Changed

- **`sandloom dev run` starts the worker and the beat when a module needs periodic jobs** (dev-run-background). A new app made `--with billing` answered `503` on checkout and portal in development until its reconciler ran once, and `dev run` started neither a worker nor a beat. Now, when the composition requires a periodic job, `dev run` runs `sandloom worker` and `sandloom beat` beside the app server with its environment, prints their output behind `worker |` and `beat |`, and stops all of them on Ctrl-C, SIGTERM or when the app server exits. A worker or beat that exits early is reported in one line while the app keeps running; on macOS a `prefork` worker refuses to start, so set `"worker_pool": "solo"` in `INFRA`. `--reload` reloads the app server only. `--no-background` serves the app alone and prints the two commands, as before. A deployment is unchanged: it runs `sandloom worker` and `sandloom beat` as processes of their own.
