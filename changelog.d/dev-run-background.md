### Changed

- **`sandloom dev run` starts the worker and the beat when a module needs periodic jobs** (dev-run-background). A new app made `--with billing` answered `503` on checkout and portal in development until its reconciler ran once, and `dev run` started neither a worker nor a beat. Now, when the composition requires a periodic job, `dev run` runs `sandloom worker` and `sandloom beat` beside the app server with its environment, prints their output behind `worker |` and `beat |`, and stops all of them on Ctrl-C, SIGTERM, a closing terminal (SIGHUP) or when the app server exits. A worker or beat that exits early is reported in one line while the app keeps running. On macOS a `prefork` worker refuses to start, so `dev run` runs its worker in the `solo` pool (the option goes into that worker's environment only; a `worker_pool` you set in `INFRA` is kept). A `dev run` that is killed outright (SIGKILL) leaves its processes running: its marker records them, and the next `dev run` of the checkout stops those that still run and says so. `--reload` reloads the app server only. `--no-background` serves the app alone and prints the two commands, as before. A deployment is unchanged: it runs `sandloom worker` and `sandloom beat` as processes of their own.
