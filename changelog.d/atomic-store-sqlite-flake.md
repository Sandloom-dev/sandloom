### Fixed

- **The `database` atomic_store driver on SQLite no longer times out a healthy write that queued behind other writers** (atomic-store-sqlite-flake). The statement deadline (`statement_timeout_seconds`, 5 s) started before a write had SQLite's single write lock, and the waiting writers of a burst polled that lock through the busy handler in no order, so on a loaded host an unlucky one spent the whole deadline waiting and raised `Timeout` (`set_subject_state` in a burst of 54 writers failed in 249 of 600 rounds under load). The writers of one process now take turns on an `asyncio.Lock` before the deadline starts; each write still gets its whole deadline, and a statement that is itself slow still raises `Timeout`. Reads and PostgreSQL take no turn. No setting changes.
