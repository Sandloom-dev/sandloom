### Changed

- **The example apps teach the current path** (dx-examples-current-path). helpdesk, projecthub, academy and linkly write through `db()` instead of an injected session, helpdesk gates its routes with `OrgRole` and `Ticket` is `TenantScoped` (migration `0005_tickets`: `organization_id` becomes `tenant_id`; the RLS policy is created only where INFRA declares `tenant_rls`, which helpdesk's does not), helpdesk's hand-added `idempotency_guard` is replaced by the derived guard with `replay_safe(reason=...)` (a keyed retry still replays the ticket body), academy's `enroll` keeps its explicit durable guard (no key refused, a retry replays the stored `EnrollmentAccepted`), and projecthub reads projects through the typed `ProjectState` DTO of `projects.read.v2` (`projects.read.v1` stays, unchanged). Helpdesk's ticket response field `organization_id` is now `tenant_id`, and a malformed organization id in a ticket URL answers 400 (the `OrgRole` gate) instead of 422. `tests/architecture/test_example_stale_markers.py` fails on a "lands with H1" / "until db()" marker or a `get_core().get_service(...)` call in an example.
