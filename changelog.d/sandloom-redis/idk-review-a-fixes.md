### Fixed

- **The `redis` atomic_store checks `maxmemory-policy` on its first operation, not only in `aopen`** (idk-review-a-fixes). A worker or CLI process never runs `aopen`, so it wrote revocation marks, blocks and idempotency reservations into an evicting Redis without a refusal. The check follows the lease's: `never_evicts_check` `verify` or `assume` behave as in `aopen`, a refusal repeats on every operation and is judged again each time, and a transient failure while reading the policy is `Unavailable` and never counts as a passed check.
- **A `noeviction` lost at runtime refuses the next operation** (idk-review-a-fixes). A `health` probe that finds the policy lost or unreadable (the lease and the atomic_store), and a lost connection (the atomic_store), open the check again. `health` of the atomic_store no longer repeats a recorded refusal without reading the server: it re-reads every time.
